# Five-model manufacturer reference scouting: evidence, provenance and rights

**Date:** 2026-11-06. **Status:** first-pass product/source qualification for gate G5; **NO media licenses, real multi-angle packs, or AYA-ready catalog entries acquired**. This is a research artifact, not a runtime dataset or a collection of images. Read [catalog sourcing](CATALOG-SOURCING.md), [system blueprint](SYSTEM-BLUEPRINT.md) and [readiness gates](DISCOVERY-GATES.md). No OEM image, CAD, PDF or client-site source bytes were copied into this repository.

## R1 / E1: What decision will this change?

**D-CAT-01:** Can Retrofit begin with a centrally bundled library of hundreds of exact, multi-angle commercial HVAC products?

- **Hypothesis A:** Manufacturer-supported product specifications and media are accessible under rights permitting app redistribution, transformation and onward transfer to the user's chosen AI service. If proven for a representative set, adopt a bounded licensed curated catalog with immutable reference versions.
- **Hypothesis B:** Technical documentation is public, but a usable pair of visual views and the necessary rights are absent or substantially conditional. If supported, do **not** build bulk media acquisition; start with owner-imported authorized references and manufacturer **links/metadata evidence** while negotiating a licensed supplier feed.
- **Discriminator:** For five equipment categories, identify an exact product/variant, official geometry/spec source, two actual images/views whose provenance is knowable, license terms or written grant for private use, *redistribution* and *external generative AI transmission*, and measure actual retrieval/QA effort. URLs and CAD downloads do not themselves constitute this proof.

**Decision so far:** B is the safer **conditional** implementation stance. Technical source coverage is promising; rights to copy the media into app releases and send it onward have **not been established** for any of the five items. G5 remains **RESEARCH NEXT/BLOCKED for bundled copyrighted assets**, while local user-authorized source import is a plausible bounded next slice. Do not represent an image from a brochure as a granted media license.

## Scope and search coverage

Primary source classes inspected: manufacturer HTML product/specification pages, manufacturer CAD/BIM and technical-download directories, specific manufacturer PDF submittal/specification versions, supplier older submittal to challenge assumed identity, current manufacturer product-line changes, and BIMobject's commercial/API access terms. Sources are links only. Manufacturer terms or permission grants for the five precise media assets were **not obtained**. Vendor contact, actual media downloads/viewer conversion, copyright legal advice, buyer account flows, physical equipment reading and trial AI transfers were **not performed**.

Discovery routes executed through web searches on 2026-11-06: each product's manufacturer + SKU + technical/dimensions, official CAD/BIM/downloads, exact controller documentation, current vs discontinued model variants, newer HTP Evo product sheets, 2026 Trane Precedent lineup/older YSC model, platform catalog/API terms and manufacturer website redistribution policies. An alternate historical document and currently active lineup were deliberately checked to challenge assumption that one model string or one product sheet is sufficient.

**Evidence class:** D1 = first-party published description/technical literature. D0 = source/discovery lead not independently accessed. D3/E2 = executed installed behavior (**none for product imports**). A D1 source can verify what a manufacturer publishes but does not prove installed-model fitness, current stock, rights or available multi-angle *licensed* images.

### Evidence caveats and correction policy

- The current Amtrol PDF page 39 was visually inspected. The historical F.W. Webb PDF text was retrieved but screenshot retrieval returned 403; the 605 mm conflicting value is therefore **text-inspected corroboration pending direct visual/vendor confirmation**. Keep both source records rather than silently normalizing one.
- Trane 2020 PDF text was inspectable; a PDF screenshot request returned a 502. Treat its numerical envelope as **candidate historical data**, not a validated current unit size.
- HTP 2025 PDF text was inspectable; its screenshot request timed out. Defer precise dimensions until its relevant sheet is visually checked or an independently viewable manufacturer HTML/manual confirms them.
- Source pages and CAD indexes confirm **published availability/links**, not that an actual CAD asset was downloaded, its version validated, rendering tested, license checked, or second independently useful angle created.
- Physical view count of **0 verified** means "no *qualified* real photo angles in this research audit", **not** a claim that the manufacturer has no photos. First-party marketing images, exploded diagrams, plans and site photos have different evidence and rights.

## Source/rights schema implications (proposed)

A future `CatalogAsset` must not collapse **family**, **model**, **variant/configuration**, **revision** or **manufacture/lifecycle period**. A future `CatalogReference` must not collapse **OEM photo**, **CAD-derived rendering**, **installation drawing**, **brochure graphic**, **field photo** and **AI conceptual illustration**. An evidence record should include:

```text
productIdentity { manufacturer, family, model, variant?, configuration?, lifecycleStatus?, effectiveDate? }
specificationClaim { sourceDocumentId, documentRevision, sourcePageOrSection, retrievedAt,
                     measuredAttribute, unit, exactValue, toleranceOrApproximation?,
                     appliesToVariant, conflictStatus }
referenceView { id, mediaType, viewKind, actualOrientation?, sourceUrl, originalBytesSha256?,
                publisher, viewIdentityProven, localStoredBytes?, sourceRevision }
rights { privateProjectStorage, appRedistribution, transformation,
         customerProposalOutput, thirdPartyAiTransfer, evidenceUrlOrGrant?,
         grantedBy?, grantDate?, attributionAndConditions?, reviewState }
```

Rights states: `unknown`, `documented_allowed`, `documented_prohibited`, `user_attested` (**not a manufacturer grant**). A source URL and a document model number may be retained for research without pretending Retrofit possesses the source bytes or a license. Separate a *technical claim's* provenance from an *image's* provenance and from the *image-sharing* permission. Preserve true historical contradictions instead of updating one canonical dimensions field without trace.

No database/JSON schema migration is authorized by this research file. First app slice can store only user-selected references and typed source labels; machine schema is a proposal to test against real inventory.

## E7 / R7: Cheapest decision-changing proof

Before shipping or developing a bulk catalog:

1. **Select one actual intended reference pack:** ideally a boiler and a tank photographed by a person authorized to use/share the images, or obtain written manufacturer permission for two exact model-specific views. Request model, variant, nameplate, source and purpose. No customer-site imagery leaves the device without an explicit approved destination.
2. **Seek explicit license language from at least two manufacturers/partners:** ask separately for local end-user storage, distribution inside an Android application, derived CAD/photo renders, client proposal use, temporary staging, onward **user-directed transmission to third-party generative AI** and required notices/expiry. Log respondent, written grant scope/version, obligations and whether subcontractors/recipients are covered. Silence is UNKNOWN, not assent.
3. **Physically inspect two independent views and dimensions:** are they genuinely of the **same exact variant** (not a family marketing montage)? Are angles orthogonal enough to inform image composition? Does one official source conflict with another? If so demand source revision or mark `geometry_uncertain`.
4. **Run a disposable link-only/manual pack trial:** choose two images with permission, import via existing Android Photo Picker path, record exact byte hashes, angle and model label, and simulate a **frozen outbound inventory only**. Do not send to external AI before recipient verification and user consent.
5. **Rerun the same proof for five equipment types** only if the first trial proves viable. Measure time per verified reference, licensing bottleneck, device storage/transfer budget and rejection count. A larger catalog is a separate business/economic decision.

**Positive oracle:** one properly authorized product with two separately inspectable model-specific views and documented permitted uses; exact bytes can be named, stored and selected without wrong-model drift. **Negative control:** a public brochure/CAD with *no* rights grant or mismatched old/new model; it must remain a source lead, not become an AI-shareable bundled reference. **Stop:** don't engineer scraper, 3D/BIM converter, 400-item store or cloud sync before the rights/content discriminator changes the architecture.
